---
title: Keep your existing number
description: Forward selected voice calls from your current provider to a Skysay AI line.
---

Keep your public business number and its current provider. Your provider or
phone-system administrator forwards the calls you choose to a separate Skysay
number. This does not port your number, import it as a Skysay-owned number, or
forward its SMS.

## Set up forwarding

Open **Numbers → Keep your number**. The four steps save a draft in your
workspace, so you can leave to prepare an agent, add funds or complete a number
request and return later.

1. Enter your business number with its country code. Choose busy, unanswered,
   unreachable, or always forwarding. Always cannot be combined with the other
   conditions. Select the AI agent that should answer.
2. Choose a dedicated Skysay line already routed to that agent. Its activation,
   agent configuration, route and available paid funds must pass the normal
   incoming-call checks. If you need another line, use the existing number
   request flow and accept its price and requirements there. Saving this setup
   never buys or reroutes a number.
3. Ask your current provider or administrator to enable the selected conditions
   and give you instructions for turning them off. Confirm destination
   compatibility and charges before renting a new destination. A provider help
   article alone is not a qualified dial-code recipe; unqualified providers get
   an administrator request rather than guessed star codes. Selecting **I've
   followed the instructions** records your report; it does not change carrier
   settings.
4. Start a test watch, then call your original business number from another
   phone. Follow the instructions for that particular condition. Enter the
   displayed six-digit test code once the AI answers. A watch lasts three
   minutes and does not place a call for you.

Your original provider may charge for the forwarded leg. Skysay usage and any
AI-line rental are billed separately. Test calls use the normal admission and
billing paths.

## Understand the result

- **No test call received:** no matching test marker reached the selected AI
  line within the watch. Check your provider settings and try again.
- **Received; forwarding unconfirmed:** the AI line received the test marker,
  but there is insufficient trusted evidence of the original number or the
  particular forwarding condition. A direct call to the AI line does not prove
  forwarding.
- **Condition verified:** both the same-call marker and qualified independent
  evidence prove the original-number path and that condition for this setup.
  Test busy and unanswered separately; one result does not verify the other.

Carrier headers are not automatically trusted. In particular, relayed
`Diversion` headers from DIDWW are unqualified until their exact provenance and
meaning have been demonstrated. Ordinary manual tests on an unqualified
provider therefore remain unconfirmed, even when you heard the AI answer.
A controlled lab test can instead retain its original-number dial record,
business-line branch trace and receiving-call evidence. It is restricted to
the isolated test workspace and configured test numbers.

Declining an incoming call is not universally a busy test. Call waiting and
carrier voicemail can affect what happens. An unanswered test must really ring
without being answered. An always-forwarded call cannot verify busy forwarding.

Editing the business number, destination, agent or conditions invalidates old
results for the current setup. History remains available. A changed route or
agent configuration requires another test. Verification is a functional
observation, not proof of ownership of a phone number.

## Stop forwarding

Use **Show how to stop forwarding**, then turn it off with your original
provider. Recording that you have done so is a user report, not a carrier
confirmation. Keep the destination line active while calls may still be
forwarded to it. This action never releases its rental automatically.

Before changing the business number or its AI destination, follow the stop
instructions and report that you have stopped forwarding. This keeps the
previous destination protected while your provider may still be sending
calls there. Changing an agent or condition requires following the updated
instructions and testing again.

There is no qualified non-AI overflow or voicemail promise in this initial
flow. If the AI line cannot accept a call, its ordinary admission behavior
applies. Arrange a backup with your provider before relying on forwarding for
critical calls.

## API and MCP

Use the same workspace-owned resources as the UI:

- `POST /v1/call-forwarding-setups` creates an idempotent draft.
- `GET /v1/call-forwarding-setups` returns a bounded, paginated list.
- `GET` and `PATCH /v1/call-forwarding-setups/{id}` read or revise a setup.
- `GET .../{id}/instructions` reads readiness and provider guidance.
- `POST .../{id}/instructions-followed` records your confirmation.
- `POST .../{id}/test-attempts` arms a test; `GET` its returned attempt ID to
  poll the result, or `POST .../{id}/test-attempts/{attempt_id}/cancel` to stop
  watching.
- `POST .../{id}/stop-report` records your report that forwarding was disabled.

Writes use `numbers:write`; reads use the scopes listed in the generated API
reference. Revision-checked writes refuse stale changes instead of overwriting
a colleague's work. MCP forwarding tools call these same services; they do not
buy lines, dial, enable forwarding or send messages implicitly.
