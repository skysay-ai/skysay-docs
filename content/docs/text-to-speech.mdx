Turn text into a WAV file with a managed Skysay voice, without an agent or a
call. Use the **Text to speech** screen in your workspace, or send one request
to `POST /v1/text-to-speech`. Both are paid from your workspace's
**purchased** balance; the welcome credit cannot pay for them.

## Generate in the workspace

Every member of the workspace can open **Voices → Text to speech**. The screen
lists only voices of the [models that support it](#models-and-languages).

1. Find a voice and select **Generate audio**.
2. Choose the **Language**. If the voice offers more than one, you can also
   choose a **Speaking style** (see [Delivery profiles](/docs/delivery-profiles)).
3. Type or paste up to 1,000 characters into **Text to speak**. The counter
   below the box shows how many you have used.
4. Select **Get price**. The button changes to **Generate for** and the exact
   price for this text, voice, language and style. If you change any of them,
   the price is cleared and you select **Get price** again.
5. Select **Generate for …** to confirm. When the audio is ready you can play
   it, select **Download WAV**, or select **Generate another**. The panel shows
   what was charged, for example "Charged $0.02 to your purchased balance."

For a Gemini voice the button reads **Generate for up to** a price, with
"Maximum charge … You pay for actual usage." You are charged for what the
provider actually used, never more than that maximum.

A walk-through, with made-up details:

> Sam manages the phone line of a small dental practice and wants a recorded
> holiday message. On **Voices → Text to speech** Sam picks an ElevenLabs
> Flash v2.5 voice, keeps English and pastes "Thank you for calling Northside
> Dental. We are closed on Friday 12 December. For urgent help, please call
> 0161 555 0142." The counter shows 120 of 1000 characters. **Get price**
> turns the button into **Generate for $0.02**. Sam selects it, but the
> workspace has only its welcome credit, so the panel says "Your purchased
> balance is too low." with a link to **Billing**. Sam tops up on Billing,
> comes back, picks the same voice and text and selects **Get price**. The
> button reads **Check attempt**, because the refused attempt is still
> remembered in this tab. Sam selects it, the button changes to
> **Generate for $0.02**, and Sam selects that, plays the result and selects
> **Download WAV**.

If the connection drops after you confirm, the panel says "The outcome is
uncertain. Check the same attempt before starting another." Select
**Check attempt**: Skysay looks up that same generation and gives you its
audio, tells you it failed and was not charged, or, if it is still running,
asks you to check again. It never generates or charges twice for one
confirmation. **Recover last WAV** fetches the most
recent WAV you generated in this browser tab, for 24 hours, without a new
charge.

### Generated audio

Open the **Generated audio** tab on the same screen, or select
**View generated audio** after a generation. It lists the clips generated in
the workspace for the current project, newest first, with the voice, provider,
model, language, length, price and the time each one is available until.
Select **Play** or **Download WAV**; neither is charged again.

Clips are kept for **24 hours** after they are generated and are then deleted.
Download any clip you want to keep. Audio generated over the API is not stored
and does not appear in this list.

## What it costs

You see the exact price before you confirm. It is worked out from your text
and the model's rate:

| Model | Billed by | Price (USD) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Azure AI Speech Neural TTS | Billable character | $0.019 per 1,000 |
| Cartesia Sonic 3.6 | Character | $0.082 per 1,000 |
| ElevenLabs Flash v2.5 | Character | $0.104 per 1,000 |
| ElevenLabs eleven_v3 | Character | $0.207 per 1,000 |
| ElevenLabs v4 Turbo | Character | $0.050 per 1,000 |
| Fish Audio S2-Pro | UTF-8 byte | $0.019 per 1,000 |
| Fish Audio S2.1-Pro | UTF-8 byte | $0.019 per 1,000 |
| Google Gemini TTS (Flash 3.8) | Input text and output audio tokens | $2 per million input tokens, $36 per million output tokens |
| Google Gemini TTS (Flash-Lite 3.8) | Input text and output audio tokens | $2 per million input tokens, $24 per million output tokens |

- The total is rounded **up** to a whole cent (or the smallest unit of your
  wallet's currency), with a minimum of one cent per generation. A EUR wallet
  is charged the converted amount at the day's rate.
- Fish Audio counts UTF-8 bytes, so a letter outside basic Latin, such as
  "ä" or "ж", counts as two or more. Azure counts each Chinese character
  twice, and each of the symbols `&`, `<`, `>`, `'` and `"` as several.
- For a Gemini model, the price shown first is the maximum for 4,096 input
  text tokens and 4,096 output audio tokens: $0.16 for Flash 3.8 and $0.11
  for Flash-Lite 3.8 in a USD wallet. After the audio is made you pay for
  the tokens the provider reports, and the rest is returned straight away.

### How the charge works

When you confirm, Skysay first holds the price from your purchased balance,
then asks the provider for the audio:

- **The audio is complete:** you are charged and any unused part of the hold
  is returned.
- **The provider fails, the audio is cut off or too large, or it takes too
  long:** the whole hold is returned and nothing is charged. The panel says
  "The render failed and was not charged. You can try again."

A hold that is not settled within two minutes is returned automatically.

### Purchased balance only

Text to speech uses **purchased** funds only. The
[welcome credit](/docs/billing#your-browser-testing-credit) cannot pay for it.

The price check does not look at your balance. A workspace with only welcome
credit, or too little purchased balance, gets a price and is refused when it
confirms: the panel says "Your purchased balance is too low." with a link to
**Billing**. Add funds on Billing, come back, get a new price and confirm
again. A request your balance cannot hold is refused at once:
[automatic recharge](/docs/billing#automatic-recharge) does not try your card
for it, so top up first. For a Gemini voice, your balance must cover the
maximum shown, not the lower amount you end up paying.

## Limits

- **Text:** 1 to 1,000 characters per generation. Text with only spaces, or
  with control characters other than line breaks and tabs, is refused.
- **Running at once:** 2 generations per workspace, and up to 12 started per
  minute. Every member and every API key in the workspace shares these
  limits. When Skysay itself is busy, a generation can also be refused for a
  few seconds. The panel says "The service is busy. Try again shortly."
- **After failures:** when 3 generations have failed within 10 minutes, new
  ones are paused for up to 10 minutes.
- **Audio:** a WAV of up to 16 MiB, about five and a half minutes of audio.
- **Workspace:** a suspended workspace cannot generate.

## Generate over the API

`POST /v1/text-to-speech` returns the finished WAV in the response. There is
no separate price step: one request holds the funds, generates the audio and
charges for it.

### Get a key with `speech:write`

The request needs an [API key](/docs/authentication) that was explicitly
granted `speech:write`. On the workspace **API keys** page, create a key and
select `speech:write` under **Scopes**. Over the API, list it by name in
`scopes` when you create a key:

```bash
curl -s -X POST "$SKYSAY_BASE_URL/v1/api-keys" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $SKYSAY_ADMIN_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"name":"Holiday messages","scopes":["speech:write"]}'
```

No [grant bundle](/docs/authentication#grant-bundles) includes `speech:write`,
not even `all` or `all-write`, so existing keys do not gain it. A key can only
pass `speech:write` to a new key if it holds `speech:write` itself.

### Send the request

Set `SKYSAY_API_KEY`, `SKYSAY_PROJECT_ID` and `SKYSAY_VOICE_ID` in your shell
and keep the key out of source control. This example uses an ElevenLabs
Flash v2.5 voice:

```bash
request_key=$(uuidgen)
jq -n --arg project "$SKYSAY_PROJECT_ID" --arg voice "$SKYSAY_VOICE_ID" '{
  project_id: $project,
  provider: "elevenlabs",
  model: "eleven_flash_v2_5",
  voice_id: $voice,
  language: "en",
  text: "Thank you for calling Northside Dental. We are closed on Friday 12 December."
}' | curl --fail-with-body --silent --show-error \
  https://api.skysay.ai/v1/text-to-speech \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $SKYSAY_API_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: $request_key" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --data-binary @- --dump-header speech-headers.txt --output speech.wav
```

| Field | Required | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `project_id` | Yes | A project in the key's workspace. |
| `provider` | Yes | `azure`, `cartesia`, `elevenlabs`, `fishaudio` or `gemini`. |
| `model` | Yes | A model ID from [Models and languages](#models-and-languages) for that provider. |
| `voice_id` | Yes | The exact ID of a managed voice of that model. |
| `language` | Yes | A language code the voice and model speak, such as `en`. |
| `text` | Yes | 1 to 1,000 characters. |
| `delivery` | No | `natural`, `calm`, `lively` or `precise`. |

The JSON body can be up to 16 KB. The `Idempotency-Key` header is required:
16 to 128 letters, digits, periods, underscores, colons or hyphens. Use a new
random value, such as a UUID, for each new generation.

A `200` response is a mono 24 kHz 16-bit PCM WAV. Its headers carry:

- `X-Skysay-Synthesis-Id`: the generation's ID.
- `X-Skysay-Charged-Minor` and `X-Skysay-Charged-Currency`: what you were
  charged, in the smallest unit of your wallet's currency. For Gemini this is
  the actual charge, not the maximum held.

Check the HTTP status before you open the saved file: an error body is JSON,
not audio.

### Repeat a request safely

Skysay does not keep audio generated over the API. Save the response; it
cannot be fetched again.

If the connection drops before you have the WAV, send the same request with the
same `Idempotency-Key`. Skysay does not generate again. It answers `409` and
tells you what happened to the first attempt:

```json
{
  "error": "ConflictError",
  "message": "This synthesis attempt already exists.",
  "reason": "speech_request_exists",
  "request_id": "tts_8KQ2m4Tz",
  "status": "succeeded"
}
```

- `succeeded`: you were charged. The audio cannot be replayed; to get it
  again, send a new request with a new key, which is charged again.
- `released`: the hold was returned and nothing was charged.
- `reserved`: the attempt is still running. Ask again shortly; a hold that is
  not settled within two minutes is returned.

A key stays tied to its attempt, in the same project, for 24 hours after the
attempt ends.

### Errors

| Status | When |
| --- | --- |
| `400` | A field is missing or invalid, the project is not in your workspace, the model is not available for text to speech, the language is not available for that model, or the `Idempotency-Key` is missing or malformed. |
| `401` | The API key is missing or invalid. |
| `402` | Your purchased balance cannot cover the price: "Insufficient purchased wallet balance for synthesis." Add funds on **Billing**. |
| `403` | The key was not granted `speech:write`. |
| `409` | The `Idempotency-Key` was already used (see above), the voice does not speak that language, or the voice is no longer available (`speech_voice_unavailable`). |
| `413` | The JSON body is larger than 16 KB. |
| `423` | The workspace is suspended. |
| `429` | A workspace or service limit, or the pause after failures, was reached. Wait for the seconds in the `Retry-After` header before trying again. |
| `503` | The audio could not be made (`speech_render_failed`) or did not finish in time (`speech_expired`); nothing was charged. Also returned when text to speech is unavailable. |

The [generated API reference](/docs/api-reference) lists every field, header
and status.
