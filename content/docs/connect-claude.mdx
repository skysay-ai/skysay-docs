description: Add Skysay to Claude as a custom connector, sign in, choose what Claude may do, and disconnect it from Connected apps.

Copy the address:

```text
https://api.skysay.ai/mcp
```

Then in Claude: **Customize → Connectors → + Add → Add custom connector**,
paste the address, **Continue**, **Add**, sign in to Skysay and choose what
Claude may do.

Claude can work in your Skysay workspace: read your calls, set up AI agents and,
when you allow it, place calls and send SMS. You add one address to Claude and
sign in to Skysay. You do not create or paste an API key.

This page is for the Claude app: claude.ai, Claude Desktop and Cowork. For the
terminal, use the [Claude Code guide](/docs/claude-code).

Claude operates Skysay for you. It is not the voice on the call: the person on
the phone hears your AI agent. To make a system you run the voice on calls, see
[Wire an agent to a number](/docs/wire-an-agent).

Claude is also not one of the models an AI agent hosted by Skysay can think
with today. The models you can choose are in the AI agent's voice settings in
the workspace, and in the `get_agent_catalog` tool
([Tool reference](/docs/tool-reference)).

## Add the connector

<Steps>
<Step>

**Open the connector settings in Claude.** Go to **Customize → Connectors**,
click **+ Add**, then **Add custom connector**.

</Step>
<Step>

**Enter the address.** Give the connector a name, such as Skysay, and enter
this server URL:

```text
https://api.skysay.ai/mcp
```

Click **Continue**, review the sign-in settings Claude detected, and click
**Add**.

</Step>
<Step>

**Sign in to Skysay.** Claude opens Skysay in your browser. Sign in with the
account that belongs to the workspace you want to connect. You can also create
an account at this step.

</Step>
<Step>

**Choose what Claude may do.** The consent screen names the app that is asking
and the workspace, and lists each permission as a checkbox. Untick anything you
do not want Claude to do, then choose **Allow access**, or **Deny** to stop.

</Step>
</Steps>

On a Claude Team or Enterprise plan an owner adds the connector once, under
**Organization settings → Connectors → Add → Custom → Web**. Each member then
opens **Customize → Connectors**, finds it and clicks **Connect**. Claude's Free
plan allows one custom connector.

## Check that it works

Ask Claude:

```text
Show my Skysay workspace overview.
```

A new workspace returns empty lists. Connecting Claude does not create an AI
agent or a phone number.

## What Claude can do

What is ticked when you choose **Allow access** is exactly what Claude gets.
The permissions Claude's tools need start ticked, including the ones that
change AI agents, place calls and send SMS, so check the list before you allow
it. Reading transcripts, recordings and SMS text has its own permissions. See
[Permissions, consent and reconnecting](/docs/mcp#permissions-consent-and-reconnecting)
for the full list and [Scopes & permissions](/docs/scopes) for what each one
allows.

Connecting Claude does not by itself approve payments, number purchases or
billable calls: you approve each paid call, SMS or number. Read
[Billing](/docs/billing) before you ask Claude to do something that costs
money.

Skysay's listing in Claude's connector directory is the
[read-only connector](/docs/mcp#read-only-connector-for-claude): five read
tools and no calls. The custom connector on this page uses the full address, so
what Claude can do is what you allow.

## Disconnect Claude

Open **Connected apps** on the [API keys](https://skysay.ai/api-keys) page in
the Skysay workspace and revoke the connection. It stops working at once. API
keys are separate and are revoked on their own.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Claude has no **Add custom connector** | On a Team or Enterprise plan only an owner can add one. Ask your owner, or use a personal plan. |
| Claude says the connector needs authentication | Open **Customize → Connectors**, select the connector and connect again, then finish the Skysay sign-in in the browser. |
| The consent screen shows the wrong workspace | Choose **Deny**, sign out of Skysay in that browser, and connect again with the right account. |
| Claude says a tool is forbidden or needs more consent | The connection lacks that permission. Connect again from **Customize → Connectors** and leave the permission ticked on the consent screen. |

Claude's menu names were checked on 20 November 2026 against Anthropic's
[custom connector guide](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11175166-getting-started-with-custom-connectors-using-remote-mcp).
If Claude's settings look different, follow that guide; the Skysay address and
sign-in stay the same.
