title: Forward calls to a phone
description: Ring your own phone on every call to a Skysay number, with no AI agent, billed as two carrier legs.

A Skysay number can forward its calls to a phone you choose, such as an office
line or a mobile, instead of answering with an AI agent. Every call to the
number rings that phone. No AI agent joins the call, so there is no recording,
transcript, summary or AI charge. You pay the two carrier legs of the call and
nothing else.

To send calls to your phone only when the AI agent cannot take them, use
[Phone backup](/docs/phone-backup) instead. To bring calls from a number you
already have to a Skysay AI line, see [Keep your number](/docs/keep-your-number).

## Set it up in the workspace

1. Open **Numbers** and select the number.
2. Under incoming call routing, choose **Forward to a phone number**.
3. Enter the phone that should ring, starting with `+` and the country code,
   then save the routing.

The phone must be:

- **In international format**, such as `+15550142`.
- **Outside Skysay.** A number on Skysay, in any workspace, cannot be a
  forwarding destination, and a number cannot forward to itself. To have
  another of your Skysay numbers' AI agents answer, route this number to that
  agent directly.
- **Not forwarding back to this number.** For example, a business line that
  your provider already forwards to this Skysay number is refused, because the
  call would go round in a loop.

A destination that breaks one of these rules is refused when you save, with
`400`, and nothing changes.

## Set it up with the API

Use `update_number_rules` or `PATCH /v1/phone-numbers/{number_id}/number-rules`
(`numbers:write`) with:

```json
{
  "incoming": {
    "when_called": "forward_to_phone_number",
    "phone_number": "+15550142"
  }
}
```

Read the saved rule back with `get_number_rules` (`numbers:read`). The
generated [API reference](/docs/api-reference) lists the exact fields. Saving
the rule places no call and reserves no money.

## What happens on a forwarded call

1. Someone calls your Skysay number.
2. Skysay checks the number's rule, your funds and your workspace's calling
   rules, and holds funds for the call (see [Pricing](#pricing)).
3. Skysay dials your phone once. Your phone shows **your Skysay number** as the
   caller, not the original caller's number. From this moment the caller's call
   is connected to Skysay, and the caller hears silence until you answer.
4. If you answer, the two of you are connected until either side hangs up or
   the call reaches 30 minutes.

Your phone rings for at most about 30 seconds, counted from when the call
arrived. If it is busy or nobody answers, the call ends: Skysay does not retry,
try another number, play a message or take a voicemail.

## In Calls

A forwarded call appears in **Calls** like any other incoming call, with no
agent, recording or transcript. Its status says how it went:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Completed | Your phone answered and the call was connected. |
| No answer | Your phone rang until the ringing time ran out. |
| Busy | Your phone line was busy. |
| Canceled | The caller hung up while your phone was ringing. |
| Failed | The call could not be connected, or a check before dialing refused it. |

A refused call, for example because your available funds cannot cover the
hold, is listed with its reason; see [Why calls fail](/docs/why-calls-fail).

## Limits

- **30 seconds of ringing** at your phone, and **30 minutes** of call.
- **One dial per call.** There is no automatic second attempt.
- **Workspace limits still apply.** A forwarded call counts toward your
  workspace's concurrent-call limit, and the call to your phone follows your
  workspace's outbound calling rules, such as its allowed destination
  countries.
- **Not on your own SIP connection yet.** A number that reaches Skysay over
  your own SIP trunk cannot forward calls; its calls are refused while the
  rule is set.

## Pricing

A forwarded call has two carrier legs, each billed by the started minute:

- **The incoming leg**, the caller's call into your Skysay number, at that
  number's incoming-call price.
- **The forwarded leg**, Skysay's call to your phone, at the outgoing price
  for that destination.

The incoming leg is billed from when Skysay starts ringing your phone, because
the caller's call is connected at that moment. A call your phone does not
answer is therefore billed one started minute of the incoming leg and nothing
for the forwarded leg. There is no AI, voice or recording charge. Prices differ
by number and destination, and the call's detail shows the price of each leg.

Before dialing, Skysay holds funds for both legs for the longest a forwarded
call can run: 30 minutes plus ringing time and a short margin, shown on the
call as 33 minutes for each leg. A hold sets money aside; it is not a charge.
After the call ends, Skysay settles each leg from its own record and returns
the unused part. If your available funds cannot cover the hold, the call is
not forwarded.
