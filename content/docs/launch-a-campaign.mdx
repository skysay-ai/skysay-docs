You need an active workspace with voice enabled, funds, a caller-ID number and
an active hosted agent. There is no campaign switch to request.

## Before you launch: test it

- **Talk to the agent in your browser**, capped at 180 seconds, for voice,
  recognition, latency and interruptions. See [Test an agent](/docs/trial-calls).
- **Run a simulation suite** in the [Simulations Lab](/docs/simulations-lab):
  scripted callers, your own pass and fail rules, text or voice.
- A [conversation workflow](/docs/agent-workflows) adds **Chat** for wording and
  branches, and **Phone**, which arms one number so your next call to it, from
  the caller ID you declared, answers with the pinned draft.
- **None of these prove the carrier route.** On a draft campaign, **Call one
  contact now** places one real, billed call to a contact you pick (add your own
  number as a row to call yourself). It uses the campaign's AI agent, caller ID
  and that contact's values, and goes through the same checks as any call:
  do-not-call, calling hours, your workspace's limits and your balance. The
  campaign stays a draft and the contact is not marked done, so the launch still
  calls them. The test call is listed on the draft with a link to its
  recording, transcript and cost. The same button is on the wizard's
  **Review and launch** step once the campaign is saved as a draft. See [Test
  call on a draft](/docs/campaigns#test-call-on-a-draft).

## Build the list

- Upload a **CSV or an Excel workbook** (`.xlsx`), one row per person, or paste
  rows. Only the phone column, in international format, is required.
- A workbook is read in your browser: the first sheet, the first row as column
  names, then the same mapping, preview and per-row report a CSV gets. Date and
  number cells arrive as the text the sheet shows — no `46280` in place of a
  date. Keep account references and phone numbers in **text-formatted columns**;
  a spreadsheet stores a number as a float and rounds anything past 15 digits
  before you export it.
- Up to 10,000 rows per import. Duplicates are skipped, invalid numbers reported
  per row.
- Every other column becomes a **variable**: 28 per contact, 512 characters
  each, 4 KB in total. No secrets.
- Time zone defaults from the number's country. Calling hours run in each
  contact's zone, or one fixed zone.
- Numbers on your [do-not-call register](/docs/campaigns#do-not-call-register)
  are marked and never dialed.

## Per-customer data in the call

- Each contact's columns become that call's [call
  variables](/docs/call-variables): whatever the agent declares is filled from
  the contact, and `contact.*` and `campaign.*` come for free. A value that does
  not fit its declaration stops that contact's call before it is placed, with
  the variable named.
- The agent's greeting, workflow lines and SMS templates speak them wherever
  you wrote `{{name}}`; a protected variable (a balance, a date of birth) is
  said only after the caller is confirmed.
- The wizard's **What this AI agent says** preview shows the chosen agent's
  greeting and lines filled in for the first contact of the list, before you
  launch. For an agent that is not ready for calls it says so instead ("This
  AI agent isn't ready for calls yet: its setup hasn't been published."), as a
  call to it would be refused.
- For a fact that must be fetched during the call, give the agent a read tool:
  an [Integration](/docs/integrations), whose URL and secret the model never
  sees, or a webhook tool.

## Create and launch

- **Contacts** — the list, the purpose, and the consent basis: existing customers
  or explicit consent.
- **Agent and numbers** — the hosted agent and the numbers to rotate caller ID
  across. Under the chosen AI agent, **How calls open** shows who speaks first,
  how long the AI agent waits for the person who answers, and its opening line,
  with an **Edit AI agent** link. A campaign has no opening or script of its
  own: what a call says comes from the AI agent, so you change it there. When
  the AI agent waits for the person to speak first and has no opening line, the
  step warns you: if the person waits too, the call can stay silent. Add an
  opening line, or let the AI agent speak first.
- **Schedule and retries** — calling hours per weekday, at most four windows a
  day, and a retry preset: sales, collections, single attempt or custom.
- **Review and launch** — contact count, spend estimate, and a preview of every
  launch check.

Launch runs one check against your workspace capabilities: no sanctioned
destination, workspace active, voice enabled, every country on your allowlist, a
permitted purpose, a declared consent basis. Caps apply per call.

## Watch the results

- The campaign page shows audience, remaining, pick-up rate, outcomes and spend.
- Each attempt opens its call: recording, transcript and any post-call
  extraction. See [Read call evidence](/docs/read-call-evidence).
- **Export CSV** gives the disposition per contact, and webhooks push
  `campaign.launched`, `campaign.contact.completed` and `campaign.completed`.
  See [Post-call results](/docs/post-call-results).

## Common questions

- **Why did my launch go to review?** Your workspace is not active, voice is not
  enabled, or a destination is not on your allowlist.
- **Why did a country need review?** It is not on your workspace's destination
  list yet.
- **Why is a contact skipped?** It is on your do-not-call register, its calling
  hours never reopen, or the campaign was cancelled. A contact whose window is
  merely closed waits for the next one.
- **Can I change settings after launch?** Pause, then change hours, caller IDs,
  retries, concurrency and end date. List, agent, purpose and consent are locked
  — duplicate to change those.
- **How many calls a day?** Whichever workspace cap binds first. The
  **Capabilities** page lists them.
- **How do I stop it?** **Pause** keeps the remaining contacts. **Cancel** ends
  the run and skips them.
- **Which lists may I call?** Your own customers, or people who explicitly
  agreed. See [Policy and outbound](/docs/policy-and-outbound#campaigns).
