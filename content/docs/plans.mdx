Every workspace starts on **pay as you go**: no monthly fee, and each call is
paid from your balance. When your calls become routine, a plan gives you a
fixed monthly price with minutes or calls included. On **Starter** you set up
your AI agent yourself; on **Professional** and **Scale** our team sets it up
and looks after it.

| Plan | Price | Included each month | Beyond that | How you get it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pay as you go | $0 a month | nothing: every call is paid from your balance | pay-as-you-go prices | [sign up](https://skysay.ai/start) |
| Starter | $49 a month or $490 a year | 300 minutes, also when paid yearly | pay-as-you-go prices | [buy it on Billing](/docs/buy-a-plan) |
| Professional | $399 a month or $3,830.40 a year | 150 calls of up to 10 minutes each, also when paid yearly | $2.00 for each extra call | [buy it on Billing](/docs/buy-a-plan) |
| Scale | from $1,000 a month | 600 calls of up to 10 minutes each | $1.50 for each extra call | [talk to us](https://skysay.ai/contact) |

Every plan has every feature and every voice, takes **3 calls at the same
time** and keeps the same [billed-number allowance](/docs/billing#billed-number-allowance)
as pay as you go. Prices are in US dollars.

## What a plan counts

- Every answered call counts, short calls and spam calls too.
- On Professional and Scale a call of up to 10 minutes counts as one call; a
  longer call counts once for every 10 minutes it started. See
  [How a long call counts](#how-a-long-call-counts).
- Starter counts each call in whole minutes: a call of 2 minutes 10 seconds
  takes 3 minutes.
- A call counts against the month it started in. The month starts on the day
  and time your plan started, in UTC, and resets every month, also on a plan
  paid yearly.
- Minutes or calls you do not use do not carry over to the next month.

### How a long call counts

On Professional and Scale a call counts by its billed time, in steps of 10
minutes:

| Billed time of the call | Counts as |
| --- | --- |
| up to 10:00 | 1 call |
| 10:01 to 20:00 | 2 calls |
| 20:01 to 30:00 | 3 calls |
| 30:01 to 40:00 | 4 calls, and so on for a longer call |

For example, on a Professional plan with 150 calls a month: a caller books a
haircut in 4 minutes, which is 1 call; a caller talks through a bridal package
for 20 minutes, which is 2 calls; a supplier call lasts 27 minutes, which is 3
calls. Billing then shows "6 of 150 calls used".

Billed time is the time every call is charged on, rounded up to whole minutes.
On an outgoing call it starts when the line is taken and can include the
ringing: a call that rings for 25 seconds and then talks for 9 minutes 50
seconds has 11 billed minutes and counts as 2 calls.

A long call can go past the end of your allowance. The part inside the
allowance is included and the rest is charged as extra calls: with 1 call
left, a 27-minute call takes that 1 call and is charged 2 extra calls, $4.00
on Professional ($3.00 on Scale). The details are in
[Billing: what a plan covers](/docs/billing#what-a-plan-covers).

## What a plan covers

Inside the allowance, the AI agent's side of a call is covered (the platform
runtime, listening, the language model, the voice and the tools it uses during
the call), and so is the carrier's charge for incoming calls. Your balance
still pays:

- the phone network's charge for outgoing calls, at the pay-as-you-go price;
- phone-number rental beyond the [one number your plan includes](#one-phone-number-included), and number setup and
  registration charges;
- SMS.

An incoming call inside the allowance needs no money on your balance: it is
answered with an empty balance and holds nothing. Outgoing calls, browser
tests and incoming calls once the allowance is used up still hold an amount
from your balance while they run. A call that goes past the allowance is
charged its extra part from your balance at the end; what the balance cannot
pay shows as **payment due**. The details, with examples, are in
[Billing: what a plan covers](/docs/billing#what-a-plan-covers).

## One phone number included

Every plan includes one phone number you rent from Skysay, up to $10 a month
of its price. A US local number (about $3 a month) or an Estonian one ($6)
costs nothing while your plan runs; a number at $80 a month costs $70. Setup
and registration fees are never included and are paid from your balance.

- **Which number:** your workspace's oldest rented number, active or still
  being set up. A number you connected yourself (imported) never takes it. An
  order you have placed holds it while its number is being set up, so a second
  number ordered meanwhile is charged in full. Numbers from an on-request offer
  (ones Skysay orders for you by hand) are charged in full; the included number
  applies to numbers ordered in the workspace.
- **Your first number at $0:** when the included amount covers the first
  month and there is no setup or registration fee, the order needs no money on
  your balance. Checkout shows the included amount as its own line.
- **One per month:** once a month's inclusion is used, it stays used that
  month, even if the number's order fails after it was charged; a replacement
  bought the same month pays that month and is included from the next. An
  order that fails before anything was charged frees it at once.
- **When the plan ends,** the number is charged in full from the next month.
  A month already charged is never charged again.
- **Every other number** pays its full monthly price from your balance; see
  [Monthly phone number charges](/docs/billing#monthly-phone-number-charges).

## Choosing a plan

- **Pay as you go** to try Skysay, or when your calls come and go.
- **Starter** when you want your phone answered every time at a price you know,
  and you set up your AI agent yourself. Buy it on **Billing**, monthly or
  yearly: [Buy a plan](/docs/buy-a-plan).
- **Professional** when you want calls handled for you without doing the
  setup. Buy it on **Billing**, monthly or yearly; paid yearly it costs
  $319.20 a month, billed $3,830.40 once a year: [Buy a plan](/docs/buy-a-plan).
- **Scale** for high call volumes at the lowest price per call. The price
  starts at $1,000 a month and is agreed with our team.

The [pricing page](https://skysay.ai/pricing) shows every plan **Yearly** or
**Monthly**: a switch above the plans moves between the two, and it opens on
Yearly, with what a year costs a month and what you save. **Get Starter
yearly** (or monthly) and **Get Professional yearly** (or monthly) take you
through sign-in to **Billing**, which opens on the side you chose, where you
buy the plan. **Talk to us** on Scale opens the contact form. A promotion
code from our team, for an amount off, a percentage off or months free, is
entered on Billing or carried by the link: [Buy a plan: with a promotion
code](/docs/buy-a-plan#with-a-code).

## Cancelling a plan

- **Starter and Professional:** cancel on **Billing** whenever you like. The
  plan runs to the end of the period you paid for, then your workspace is back
  on pay as you go. See [Buy a plan: cancel](/docs/buy-a-plan#cancel).
- **A plan agreed with our team** (Scale, or any plan on terms we agreed with you):
  a plan you accepted and paid on Billing is cancelled there, as Starter is. A
  plan our team put you on without a payment on Billing ends as you agreed
  with us.

## The pricing page's calculator

The calculator on the pricing page estimates **pay as you go** only: it prices
every call at pay-as-you-go rates, and a plan's included minutes or calls are
not in it. The same goes for `POST /v1/pricing/estimate`, which refuses a plan
id (`starter`, `professional`, `scale`) with `400`.

## The plans in the public catalogue

`GET /v1/pricing/catalog` needs no key. Its `public_price_book` lists pay as
you go in `plans` and Starter, Professional and Scale in `paid_plans`, the same
figures the pricing page shows. Each entry in `paid_plans` has:

- `prices`: the fee for each billing `interval` (`month`, and `year` for
  Starter and Professional), in US cents (`fee_minor`); a yearly fee already
  carries its saving; `price_from` is `true` when the fee is a starting price
  (Scale);
- `allowance_unit` (`minute` or `call`) and `allowance_per_month`;
- `overage`: `pay_as_you_go` (beyond the allowance a call is charged at
  pay-as-you-go prices) or `per_call`, with its price in
  `overage_unit_price_minor`;
- `purchase`: `workspace` (bought on Billing) or `talk_to_us`;
- `included_concurrent_calls`: the calls a workspace on the plan takes at the
  same time.

The [OpenAPI document](/docs/openapi) describes the operation.
