Every finished call in your workspace can carry one **failure label**: why the
call went wrong, and which side caused it. The label shows as a chip on the
Calls page, in full in the call's drawer, as a breakdown on a campaign, and for
the last 30 days on each AI agent's overview.

A call with no label chip had **no failure observed**. That is not the same as
proof that the call went well: some failures can only be detected when the
call's full evidence (its transcript and its timing telemetry) has arrived.

The label is not the reason the call *ended*. Every call also reports why it
ended (the caller hung up, the AI agent finished, a safety rule ended it); see
[Post-call results](/docs/post-call-results#why-a-call-ended). The failure label
reads that reason, the line's own result and the call's evidence, and says
whether anything went wrong.

## Three places a call can fail

| Layer | What happened | Who acts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Telephony** | The call never became a conversation. | You: check the contact list, the calling times or the number. |
| **Audio and media** | A conversation started and Skysay's voice pipeline broke. | Skysay: the failure is ours. |
| **AI agent behaviour** | The pipeline worked; the AI agent or one of your own systems failed. | You: check the AI agent's prompt, your webhook or tool, or the transfer number. |

When a call shows more than one problem, the label names the root cause: a
call that never connected is a telephony failure whatever else happened, and a
broken pipeline explains anything the AI agent did wrong on that call. The
other problems observed stay visible in the call's drawer.

## The labels

### Telephony

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Inbound call rejected | Skysay's admission refused the incoming call before it was answered, for example because the workspace could not take it. |
| Invalid number | The number does not exist or cannot be dialled. |
| Rejected by the carrier | The carrier refused the call. |
| Line busy | The line was busy. |
| No answer | Nobody answered before the ring timed out. |
| Answered by a machine | The line was answered, but only by a machine, music or tones, never a person. |
| Telephony technical failure | The call failed before it could connect, for a technical reason on the line. |

### Audio and media

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Media session lost | The audio session went away during the call. |
| Voice engine lost | The conversation engine closed underneath the call. |
| Reply stalled | The AI agent stopped replying while the caller was talking to it, and the call was ended. |
| Caller speech missed | The call was ended for no caller speech although the caller did speak. |
| AI agent went silent | The caller spoke and the AI agent never replied. |
| Speech recognition stalled | Recognising what the caller said failed or timed out. |
| Language model failed | The language model failed or timed out on a reply. |
| Speech synthesis failed | Turning the reply into speech failed or timed out. |
| Goodbye cut off | The AI agent's closing line was cut before it finished playing. |

### AI agent behaviour

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Transfer went wrong | A transfer to a person failed, was rejected, or its outcome never came back. |
| Text message not sent | An SMS the AI agent tried to send during the call failed. |
| Tool call failed | A tool the AI agent called (your webhook or integration) failed or timed out. |

## Limited evidence

Some labels rest on the call's transcript and timing telemetry: *Caller speech
missed*, *AI agent went silent*, *Speech recognition stalled*, *Language model
failed*, *Speech synthesis failed*, *Goodbye cut off*, *Tool call failed* and
*Text message not sent*. Skysay only uses them when that evidence is complete,
because on partial evidence a missing turn may be a gap in the evidence rather
than something that happened.

When a call connected but its evidence is incomplete, the call's drawer says
so: *Limited evidence: only carrier and session problems can be detected for
this call.* Labels are decided again as evidence arrives, so a call can gain a
more specific label a few moments after it ends.

## Finding failed calls

On the Calls page, the failure filter narrows the list to one layer or one
label; the count and "Load more" follow the filter. A campaign's detail page
breaks its calls down by layer and label, and each AI agent's overview shows the
same breakdown for its calls from the last 30 days. When there is nothing to
show, the breakdown says *No failures detected* rather than drawing zeroes.
