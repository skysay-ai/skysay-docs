Connect your Pipedrive account to one Skysay workspace and project. Choose an
AI agent and an active caller-ID number, then start calls from a person's phone
number in Pipedrive. Opening the calling window never places a call on its own.

## Connect your accounts

1. Sign in to Skysay and open **Integrations → Connect Pipedrive**.
2. Authorize the Pipedrive application. It requests basic account information,
   read-only Contacts and Deals access, and Call logs access. Contacts and Deals
   are read to verify the selected person, phone number and deal; they are not
   edited. Call logs access creates the resulting call activity.
3. Confirm the named Pipedrive company and user in your Skysay workspace.
4. Select your project’s AI agent, caller-ID number, call purpose and recipient
   authorization, then save the call settings.

A Pipedrive user/company connects to one workspace/project at a time. Each
Pipedrive user authorizes their own connection. Disconnect the old workspace
before moving it to another. Access and refresh tokens stay encrypted on the
server; they are never sent to the calling window.

You need a configured AI agent, an active phone number and sufficient purchased
balance for telephone calls. Normal Skysay calling permissions, destination
rules, concurrent-call limits and billing apply.

## Call from Pipedrive

Choose Skysay as your Pipedrive calling method. Open a person's phone number,
check the name and international phone number in the Skysay window, then choose
**Confirm and call**. A deal entry must belong to that person. A missing or
mismatched contact/number is refused before call admission.

The call starts in the AI agent’s configured default language. The AI agent
calls the contact over the telephone. You are not joining a browser
softphone call. Keep the calling window open to follow progress; closing it does
not cancel an admitted call. You can manage the call in Skysay's Calls page.

## Find the result

The result is saved as a Pipedrive call activity on the original person and,
when selected, the original deal. It includes outcome, connected duration and
a link to the Skysay call. Delivery waits up to two minutes for a pending summary. A summary is included
if one is ready by then. Recordings and full transcripts remain in Skysay and require
workspace access; this connector does not upload recording files to Pipedrive.

Delivery continues on the server even if the calling window closes. Duplicate
confirmation requests reuse the original Skysay call. If Pipedrive's response is
lost after it may have created an activity, the result is marked for attention
rather than posted again. Contact support to reconcile it before retrying.

## Disconnect

Choose **Disconnect** in Skysay's Pipedrive settings or uninstall the app in
Pipedrive. Disconnecting invalidates the calling window and stops future calls
and pending result deliveries from this connection. A request already in flight
may finish. Calls already admitted remain in Skysay, and existing Pipedrive
activities are retained. Reconnect to restore access after an expired or revoked
authorization.

This connector handles user-confirmed outbound AI calls. Inbound caller
identification, automatic CRM synchronization and SMS actions are not part of
this calling window.
