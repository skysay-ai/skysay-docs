## Hear your agent in the browser

Open **Agents**, select your agent, configure its supported STT, LLM, and TTS
components, then start a browser test. Allow microphone access and end the
session when you finish. Each session is capped at 180 seconds.

Authenticated browser tests use [welcome browser credit](/docs/billing) or
purchased funds at the selected components' metered prices. You do not need a
phone number for a browser test. This does not qualify the carrier route or
prove that a real phone call will work.

## Test a real phone call

For a PSTN test, use a normally configured number and carrier route, purchased
funds, and the usual authorization and policy checks. Browser credit cannot
fund the phone call. See [Wire an agent](/docs/wire-an-agent) and
[Policy and outbound](/docs/policy-and-outbound).

## Call a homepage demo

To hear an OpenPhonex example over the phone without setting up a workspace,
open the [homepage](https://openphonex.com/) and choose **Call the number** when
that option is available. Dial the displayed number. Its language label describes
the agent answering that number, not the language selected for the website.

You do not need an OpenPhonex account, a subscription, or a wallet topup for
this example. OpenPhonex funds its own demo workspace; your telephone provider
may charge you for the call, including international charges. Your $5 welcome
browser credit is separate and cannot pay for phone calls.

These are short, shared demonstrations, not a way to connect your own agent
to a public number. A protected inbound demo ends after at most 180 seconds
from admission, including connection setup. Capacity and daily admission-budget
limits can prevent new calls; seeing a number on the page does not reserve a
slot. The AI can converse and end the call, but cannot transfer you, send a
message, or perform an external action. There is no automatic callback.

The demonstration does not prove that your own carrier route or agent is ready.
Use a normally funded call for that test.

## Shared-number PIN trials are retired

New customer-configured live shared-number trials are not available. A PIN from
`create_trial_call` is **not** a number you can dial to reach your agent.

The tool and `POST /v1/trial-calls` remain for offline synthetic fixtures and
require `synthetic_only: true`. They do not create real audio or prove a working
phone route. Live routing/completion through the old trial path is rejected.
Existing trial records remain readable. This change does not remove the
separately bounded anonymous homepage demo.

## Review the result

Use **Calls** or the call-evidence tools to inspect status and available
recordings and transcripts. A failed call or a synthetic fixture need not have
real audio evidence. See [Read call evidence](/docs/read-call-evidence).
