A public link is an address such as `skysay.ai/talk/your-address`. Anyone who
opens it can talk to that AI agent in their browser. They do not need an
account or a phone.

The public link is the AI agent itself. It uses the agent's published
instructions, voice, languages, knowledge and workflow, and the tools the
agent has. Those tools are real: if the agent can book appointments, a visitor
books a real appointment. To offer less to strangers, make a separate AI agent
with fewer tools and share that one.

## Switch the link on

You need permission to edit AI agents. You can manage the link in the
workspace, through the REST API, or with MCP.

1. Open **Agents**, select the AI agent, and open **Share**.
2. The **Address** field starts with an address made from the agent's name.
   Keep it or type another: 3 to 40 lowercase letters, digits and hyphens,
   with no hyphen at the start or end and no two hyphens in a row. Capitals
   become lowercase and spaces become hyphens as you type. From the third
   character the field shows **Available**, **Taken**, **This address is
   reserved** or **Your address**.
3. Turn **Public link** on. The first time, this saves the address with it.

To change the address later, type the new one and choose **Save**. Turning
the switch off and on again keeps the saved address.

**Copy link** copies the full address. The **X**, **LinkedIn**, **WhatsApp**
and **Telegram** buttons open each network's own share page with the link.
While the link is off they are disabled. **Preview as visitor** opens the
public page in a new tab; it appears once the link is saved and on.

Each address belongs to one AI agent across all of Skysay, and an AI agent
has one address. Turning the link off keeps the address for you: the page
then answers as not available, and nobody else can take it. Saving a different
address frees the old one. Some addresses that read as Skysay's own pages are
reserved.

Only an AI agent that Skysay hosts can have a public link. A webhook agent or
a realtime audio agent cannot.

## Manage the link with REST or MCP

| Action | REST | MCP | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Read the current link or check a proposed address | `GET /v1/agents/{agent_id}/public-link` | `get_agent_public_link` | `agents:read` |
| Publish, turn off, or rename the link, or change its limits | `PATCH /v1/agents/{agent_id}/public-link` | `set_agent_public_link` | `agents:write` |

Use a credential for the AI agent's workspace. MCP takes `agent_id` in
the tool arguments. To check an address, pass `check` as a query parameter
in REST or an argument to the MCP read tool.

Use the same fields in the PATCH body or MCP write arguments:

- To publish for the first time, send `{"slug":"front-desk","enabled":true}`.
- To make the link private, send `{"enabled":false}`. This keeps the address
  reserved and affects public browser access only.
- To rename, send `{"slug":"new-address"}`. The link stays public or private
  as it was, and another workspace can claim the previous address.
- To publish again at a saved address, send `{"enabled":true}`.
- To change the [limits](#limits), send `daily_conversations_per_visitor`
  (1 to 1000, or `null` for no limit) and `concurrent_conversations`
  (1 to `max_concurrent_conversations`, which the read returns), together or
  on their own: `{"daily_conversations_per_visitor":null,"concurrent_conversations":4}`.
  A value outside its range is refused with `400` and changes nothing. A link
  needs an address before its limits can be set.

A successful change returns the saved settings. `link.path` is relative to
`https://skysay.ai`, and `link` is null until an address is saved. An enabled
link lets anonymous visitors start browser conversations billed to your workspace.

## What visitors see

The page shows the AI agent's name, that it is an AI voice agent, the
languages it speaks, and a notice that the conversation is recorded, shared
with your business and lasts up to 5 minutes. The visitor presses
**Start talking** and allows the microphone.

- Each conversation lasts up to 5 minutes, then ends on its own. The page
  counts down the time left.
- The languages shown are those of the published AI agent. If it follows
  the caller's language, the page lists every language it can switch to and
  it switches here too; otherwise the page lists the one language it talks in.
- Tools that need a phone line, such as sending an SMS, transferring to a
  person or saving a callback, do not work in a browser conversation.
  Calendar booking does.
- A **Share** block with **Copy link** and the four networks stays on the
  page. After the conversation the page offers **Talk again** and
  **Build your own AI agent**.
- An address that does not exist, or a link that is switched off, shows a
  plain "This link is not available" page.

The page is not listed in search engines.

## Calls and billing

Every public link conversation is an ordinary recorded call of your
workspace. It appears in **Calls** with the type **Public link**, and the
transcript names the other speaker **Visitor**. With **Real calls** selected,
choose **Public link** in the **Call connection** list to show only these
calls. The recording and transcript work as for any other call.

Conversations are billed to your balance at the normal browser-call rates.
Welcome browser credit does not pay for them. Each conversation holds the
cost of the full 5 minutes from your balance when it starts and is charged
for the time actually used. If your balance cannot cover a new conversation
and automatic recharge is on, Skysay recharges your balance as it does for
your other calls, then starts the conversation. If the balance still cannot
cover it, for example because automatic recharge is off or the payment did
not go through, the visitor is told the AI agent is not available right now,
and no call is created.

## Limits

Each link has two limits you can change, and Skysay keeps one more:

- **Conversations per visitor, per day:** 10 until you change it. Raise it up
  to 1000, or choose **No limit**. The daily limit counts each link
  separately: the conversations one visitor starts on this link in a day
  (UTC), whatever they do on other links.
- **Conversations at the same time:** 3 until you change it, up to 4. Every
  conversation runs on the same voice capacity as phone calls, which is why
  Skysay keeps a ceiling.
- **2 at the same time per visitor**, across all your public links together.
  This one is not a setting.

To change them, open **Share**. Once the link has an address, a line there
states the current limits; choose **Change**, set the two numbers (or tick
**No limit**), and choose **Save**.
Turning the link off and on again, or changing its address, keeps them.

Every conversation is billed to your balance, so a higher limit can cost
more. With **No limit**, nothing but your balance stops one visitor starting
conversation after conversation; each one still ends after 5 minutes.

Skysay tells visitors apart by their network address, so people behind one
office connection count as one visitor. A visitor over a limit sees "This AI
agent is busy right now. Try again in a few minutes." and never learns which
limit it was. The daily count resets at midnight UTC.

Public link conversations share Skysay's voice lines with phone calls and
browser tests. When every line is in use, a new conversation does not start:
within a few seconds the visitor sees the same busy message. That
conversation is listed in **Calls** as canceled and is not charged. It does
not count toward the visitor's daily limit, and it frees its place on the
link at once.

The page says the AI agent is not available right now when the agent is
stopped or suspended, your workspace is suspended, Skysay has paused your
workspace's inbound calls, or during scheduled maintenance.
