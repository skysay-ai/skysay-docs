title: Share an AI agent with a public link
description: Give one AI agent an address anyone can open to talk to it in the browser, with no account. Conversations are recorded, appear in Calls and are billed to your balance.

---

A public link is an address such as `skysay.ai/talk/your-name`. Anyone who
opens it can talk to that AI agent in their browser. They do not need an
account or a phone.

The public link is the AI agent itself. It uses the agent's published
instructions, voice, languages, knowledge and workflow, and it runs the
agent's real tools. If the agent can book appointments, a visitor can book a
real appointment. To offer less to strangers, make a separate AI agent with
fewer tools and share that one.

## Switch the link on

1. Open **Agents**, select the AI agent, and open **Share**.
2. Type an address. It uses 3 to 40 lowercase letters, digits and hyphens.
   From the third character the field tells you whether the address is
   available, taken or not allowed.
3. Turn **Public link** on. The address is saved with it.

**Copy link** copies the full address. The buttons for X, LinkedIn,
WhatsApp and Telegram open each network's own share window with the link.
**Preview as visitor** opens the public page in a new tab.

Each address belongs to one AI agent across all of Skysay. Turning the link
off keeps the address for you: the page then answers as not available, and
nobody else can take it. Saving a different address frees the old one.

Only an AI agent that Skysay hosts can have a public link.

## What visitors see

The page shows the AI agent's name, that it is an AI voice agent, the
languages it speaks and a notice that the conversation is recorded. The
visitor presses **Start talking** and allows the microphone.

- Each conversation lasts up to 5 minutes, then ends on its own.
- The languages shown are those of the published AI agent. If it follows
  the caller's language, the page lists every language it can switch to and
  it switches here too; otherwise the page lists the one language it talks in.
- Tools that need a phone number, such as sending an SMS, transferring to a
  person or saving a callback, are not available in a browser conversation.
- After the conversation the page offers to try again, to share the link,
  and to build an AI agent of your own.
- An address that does not exist, or a link that is switched off, shows a
  plain "This link is not available" page.

The page is not listed in search engines.

## Calls and billing

Every public link conversation is an ordinary recorded call of your
workspace. It appears in **Calls** with the type **Public link** and the
other speaker as **Visitor**. Choose **Public link** under **Call connection**
to list only these calls. The recording and transcript work as for any other
call.

Conversations are billed to your balance at the normal browser-call rates.
Welcome browser credit does not pay for them. If your balance cannot cover a
new conversation and automatic recharge is on, Skysay recharges your balance
as it does for your other calls, then starts the conversation. If the balance
still cannot cover it, for example because automatic recharge is off or the
payment did not go through, the visitor is told the AI agent is not available
right now, and no call is created.

Skysay limits how many conversations run at the same time, per visitor and
per link, so one busy link cannot take all of your capacity. A visitor over
the limit is asked to try again in a few minutes. A suspended workspace or AI agent,
a pause Skysay puts on your workspace's inbound calls, and scheduled
maintenance stop public link conversations too.
