**Call with Skysay** is a Chrome pilot for workspaces with extension access.
Use the extension package provided with your pilot invitation. You need a
signed-in Skysay workspace, a published hosted AI agent, and a caller ID that
can place outbound calls. The usual workspace permissions, destination rules
and call charges apply.

## Call a number

1. In Chrome, select a number and choose **Review number in Skysay** from the
   right-click menu. You can also open the extension from the toolbar and
   enter the number. To show phone buttons on a website, choose **Enable
   icons on this site** and approve Chrome's site permission.
2. Click **Connect**. If Skysay opens a sign-in tab, sign in there and then
   return to the extension and click **Connect** again.
3. Choose the AI agent and caller ID. Check the international destination
   shown in the panel. For a local number, select the country to use.
4. Click **Call with AI**. Your selected agent speaks on the call. Follow the
   call status in the panel or use **Open call** to see it in Skysay.

If the extension cannot confirm a request, use its retry action to continue the
same request. Check the Calls page before placing a separate call.

## Save contacts as a draft

On a page with contacts, open the extension and click **Review page numbers**.
Review the names and numbers from the current view, correct them if needed,
and select the people to include. The panel shows up to 50 detected numbers.
Choose an agent, caller ID, campaign name, purpose and consent basis, then
click **Save draft campaign**.

Use **Open draft** to review the saved list in Skysay. Start calling only
after you review the draft and explicitly launch it there. See
[Campaigns](/docs/campaigns) for calling hours and consent requirements.

The extension finds candidates in the browser. It sends the confirmed
destination for a call or the rows you select for import. To remove the phone
buttons from a site, return to that site and click **Disable icons on this site**.
Signing out of Skysay ends access through that session.

If Chrome blocks the connection even though you are signed in, check that
your browser permits your Skysay session for the extension. Do not paste an
API key into a webpage or the panel. Reconnect when the short-lived
connection expires.
