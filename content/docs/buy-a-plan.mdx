A plan gives your workspace a monthly allowance for its AI agents' calls. You
can buy **Starter** yourself on **Billing**. **Professional** and **Scale**
include setup by our team: choose **Talk to us**, and once we have agreed the
terms with you, we offer you the plan on **Billing**, where you accept it and
pay through Stripe. See [Accept an offer from our team](#accept-an-offer-from-our-team).

What a plan covers, and what your balance still pays for, is explained in
[Billing](/docs/billing).

## Buy Starter

1. Open **Billing** and find **Plans**.
2. Choose **Choose monthly** or **Choose yearly** under Starter. You go to
   Stripe's payment page, where you enter your card and your billing details.
   The plan is paid with the card you enter there; it is kept apart from the
   card saved for top-ups and automatic recharge.
3. Pay. You come back to Billing, which says **Activating Starter** until
   Stripe confirms the payment, usually within seconds. The page then shows
   your plan, its price and the date of the next payment.

Your plan starts at the moment Stripe confirms the payment, not when you come
back to the page. If you close Stripe's page without paying and choose the
same option again, the same page opens: you are never asked to pay twice. If
you choose the other interval instead, the first page is closed before the
new one opens. An unpaid page closes after an hour.

You can buy a plan only while your workspace has none. To change plans,
cancel and choose again after the paid period ends, or talk to us.

## Accept an offer from our team

When our team agrees a plan with you, for example Professional at a launch
price or on a 12-month term, we offer it to your workspace. You see it on
**Billing → Plans**, above the other plans, as **Your offer from Skysay**: the
plan, its price, its monthly allowance, the price of each call beyond the
allowance and, when one was agreed, the term.

1. Check the terms, then choose **Accept and pay**. You go to Stripe's payment
   page, as for Starter, where you enter your card and your billing details.
2. Pay. You come back to Billing, which says **Activating** until Stripe
   confirms the payment. Your plan starts at that moment, with exactly the
   terms of the offer, whatever the plan's list price is later.

Nothing changes until you pay: the offer alone starts no plan and charges
nothing. If you close Stripe's page without paying, the offer stays on Billing
and you can accept it again. From the first payment on, the plan renews,
retries a failed payment and cancels exactly as Starter does (see below).

- **A yearly offer** is paid for the year up front and renews every year at the
  same price until you cancel. Your allowance still resets every month.
- **A term** (for example 12 months) is shown on the offer and on your plan for
  your information. Cancelling works as for Starter: the plan runs to the end
  of the period you paid for.
- Offers are in US dollars, whatever the currency of your balance.
- Our team can withdraw an offer you have not paid yet, for example to offer
  you other terms. Once you have paid on Stripe's page, the plan starts.
- While your workspace is on a plan, no offer can be accepted.

## Prices are in US dollars

Plans are billed in US dollars, whatever the currency of your balance. A
workspace with a euro balance pays the plan in dollars on Stripe's page and
keeps paying calls, phone numbers and SMS in euros from its balance. Buying a
plan never adds money to your balance and never turns on automatic recharge.

## Your balance still matters

Every call still needs funds in your balance for its hold when it starts,
also inside your plan's allowance. When your purchased balance is below the
smallest top-up and automatic recharge is off, **Plans** on Billing offers
**Add funds** and, separately, **Turn on auto-recharge**. Neither is
required to buy a plan.

## Renewal

Stripe charges your card at the start of each paid period, every month or
every year. Your plan carries on without a break, and your allowance keeps
resetting every month on the day and time your plan started, also on a
yearly plan.

## When a payment fails

If a renewal payment fails, your plan stays while Stripe tries the payment
again over the next days. Billing shows that the last payment failed, with
**Pay the invoice** to pay it with another card, and the workspace's owners
get one email for that invoice. If every attempt fails, the plan ends and
your workspace is on pay as you go.

## Cancel

On **Billing → Plans**, choose **Cancel plan** and confirm. Your plan stays
until the end of the period you paid for; Billing shows, for example,
"Starter until 24 Mar, then pay as you go". From that date your workspace is
on pay as you go, and you can buy a plan again. A cancellation cannot be
undone from Billing; choose the plan again after it ends.

A plan you accepted from our team's offer and paid through Stripe is cancelled
the same way. A plan our team put you on without a payment on Stripe's page is
changed or ended by talking to us.
