## Pay as you go

PAYG has **no monthly platform subscription**. Add purchased funds when you
need phone calls, SMS, phone numbers, or more browser-testing usage. A wallet
top-up is a one-time purchase, not a recurring plan subscription.

Core agents, supported provider choices, tools, knowledge bases, API, MCP,
webhooks, and standard call evidence do not require a paid platform plan.
Usage is still metered. Phone-number rental and setup charges are separate;
an imported number's carrier may bill you directly. A hosted agent on
[GPT-Live 1](/docs/conversation-engines) is billed by call duration, from the
moment the call is placed, at $0.05 per minute, including silence, plus tokens
for the backend that handles tools.

PAYG's standard workspace allowance is **10 billed phone numbers** and
**10 simultaneous live calls**, shared across projects and agents. Number
rental and setup are charged separately; this is permission to hold up to ten
paid numbers, not ten free numbers. Carrier-billed imports and zero-rental
numbers do not consume the billed-number allowance. Owning more numbers does
not multiply the concurrent-call allowance.

For more than 10 billed numbers or 10 simultaneous calls, **[contact us](https://skysay.ai/contact)**
about **Scale**. An Skysay operator can set a workspace's concurrent-call
limit below or above the plan allowance. A value of `0` resets that control to
the plan default; it does not disable calls. Choosing or changing a use-case
profile does not change this concurrency control. Other workspace-specific
risk limits or agreed overrides can change the effective allowance, and active historical subscriptions retain their
recorded plan capacity. Available funds, carrier capacity, and other admission
checks still apply. This allowance is not a guarantee of ten connected calls.

Higher capacity must be agreed and validated for your workload; do not assume
a higher limit from a pricing label. Check your workspace's current limits
before launching a batch of calls.

## Billed-number allowance

Open **[Phone numbers](https://skysay.ai/numbers)** or
**[Billing](https://skysay.ai/billing)** to see your workspace's billed-number
allowance: used, limit, and remaining. This is workspace-wide across projects,
not just the numbers on the current page. Only numbers with an Skysay
monthly rental count; carrier-billed imports and zero-rental numbers do not.
Rental and setup charges are separate from the allowance.

Checkout displays the allowance before confirmation and disables confirmation
for an additional billed number when the limit is reached. Use **Request more
capacity** to contact us. Adding wallet funds does not increase the number limit.
If the allowance is unavailable, refresh before ordering a billed number.

The server also checks capacity before starting a new managed carrier purchase
or reserving its payment. The display is a current count, not a capacity
reservation while you browse. Other verification and availability checks still
apply. Previously purchased orders continue through reconciliation rather than
being purchased again.

## Your browser-testing credit

An eligible customer receives a **one-time USD $5 welcome credit** when signing
into the workspace. For a EUR wallet, the grant is converted using the rate
recorded at grant time. The credit has **no expiry** and is limited to one grant
per customer identity and per workspace. Creating another workspace or inviting
another member does not replenish an already claimed grant.

Use it from **Agents → select an AI agent → Talk to it**, which opens the test
panel beside the editor. Configure a supported STT, LLM, and TTS stack first. Each authenticated browser session is capped at
180 seconds and uses the selected components' metered prices. This is a credit
balance, not a promise of a fixed number of free minutes or unlimited tests.

Browser sessions reserve funds before starting. Unused reserved funds are
released when the session settles. Once browser credit is exhausted, further
tests require purchased funds. A negative purchased balance must be resolved;
browser credit does not cancel a debt.

<Callout title="Browser credit is not phone credit">
  Welcome credit cannot pay for PSTN calls, SMS, phone-number rental or setup.
  It is not cash, refundable purchased credit, or transferable to another
  workspace. The anonymous homepage demo is a separate, bounded demo; it does
  not replenish your workspace credit.
</Callout>

## Reading your balance and costs

The workspace wallet balance shows **purchased funds** available for phone
usage. Billing shows browser credit separately. A wallet showing $0 purchased
funds can therefore still have browser-testing credit available.

The agent editor names the credit before a browser test only while there is
credit left to spend. Once the grant is gone, the note there stops naming a
payer rather than claiming a balance you no longer have.

For API clients, read the wallet with `GET /v1/wallet` (scope `wallet:read`).
For MCP clients, the `account_overview` tool (scope `account:read`) returns a
combined workspace rollup whose `wallet` object carries the same balance
fields. Either surface separates `available_minor` (purchased),
`promotional_available_minor` (browser credit), and `total_available_minor`
(both). Amounts are in the wallet currency's minor units. Do not treat the
combined total as a PSTN spending balance.

Call charges depend on the selected components and their actual usage, not a
flat all-inclusive price per minute. Inspect the call's cost evidence for the
components used. BYOK provider charges follow that component's billing mode;
bringing a provider key does not make unrelated platform or carrier usage free.
New calls use their own pricing snapshot. Existing call prices and historical
wallet entries are not rewritten when the catalog changes.

## Existing subscription records

Developer, Launch, and Growth monthly/yearly subscriptions are no longer sold.
If your account already has a subscription record, Billing retains its status
and cancellation controls. Switching the public offer to PAYG does not itself
cancel a Stripe subscription or refund an earlier payment. Check Billing before
assuming an existing recurring charge has stopped.

## Call amounts on Overview

For component-metered calls, Recent calls uses the same wallet billing verdict
as Calls. Once settlement is available, the amount is the settled charge, not
the larger amount reserved at admission. Released holds show **Released**;
reservations awaiting usage remain explicitly labelled as reserved. A
**payment due** result separates the amount paid from the outstanding amount.

Overview spend totals combine settled charges with still-pending reservations
and SMS charges. Released reservations and unpaid balances do not count as
settled spend. Legacy calls without a component-billing verdict retain their
existing cost calculation.
