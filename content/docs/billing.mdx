## Pay as you go

PAYG has **no monthly platform subscription**. Add purchased funds when you
need phone calls, SMS, phone numbers, or more browser-testing usage. A wallet
top-up is a one-time purchase, not a recurring plan subscription.

Core agents, supported provider choices, tools, knowledge bases, API, MCP,
webhooks, and standard call evidence do not require a paid platform plan.
Usage is still metered. Phone-number rental and setup charges are separate;
an imported number's carrier may bill you directly.

For higher capacity or volume terms, contact sales about **Scale**. Capacity
must be agreed and validated for your workload; do not assume a higher limit
from a pricing label. Check your workspace's current limits before launching
a batch of calls.

## Your browser-testing credit

An eligible customer receives a **one-time USD $5 welcome credit** when signing
into the workspace. For a EUR wallet, the grant is converted using the rate
recorded at grant time. The credit has **no expiry** and is limited to one grant
per customer identity and per workspace. Creating another workspace or inviting
another member does not replenish an already claimed grant.

Use it from **Agents → select an agent → browser test**. Configure a supported
STT, LLM, and TTS stack first. Each authenticated browser session is capped at
180 seconds and uses the selected components' metered prices. This is a credit
balance, not a promise of a fixed number of free minutes or unlimited tests.

Browser sessions reserve funds before starting. Unused reserved funds are
released when the session settles. Once browser credit is exhausted, further
tests require purchased funds. A negative purchased balance must be resolved;
browser credit does not cancel a debt.

<Callout title="Browser credit is not phone credit">
  Welcome credit cannot pay for PSTN calls, SMS, phone-number rental or setup.
  It is not cash, refundable purchased credit, or transferable to another
  workspace. The anonymous homepage demo is a separate, bounded demo; it does
  not replenish your workspace credit.
</Callout>

## Reading your balance and costs

The workspace wallet balance shows **purchased funds** available for phone
usage. Billing shows browser credit separately. A wallet showing $0 purchased
funds can therefore still have browser-testing credit available.

For API and MCP clients, read the account overview using `account:read`:
`GET /v1/account/overview` or the `account_overview` MCP tool. The wallet
separates `available_minor` (purchased), `promotional_available_minor` (browser
credit), and `total_available_minor` (both). Amounts are in the wallet currency's
minor units. Do not treat the combined total as a PSTN spending balance.

Call charges depend on the selected components and their actual usage, not a
flat all-inclusive price per minute. Inspect the call's cost evidence for the
components used. BYOK provider charges follow that component's billing mode;
bringing a provider key does not make unrelated platform or carrier usage free.
New calls use their own pricing snapshot. Existing call prices and historical
wallet entries are not rewritten when the catalog changes.

## Existing subscription records

Developer, Launch, and Growth monthly/yearly subscriptions are no longer sold.
If your account already has a subscription record, Billing retains its status
and cancellation controls. Switching the public offer to PAYG does not itself
cancel a Stripe subscription or refund an earlier payment. Check Billing before
assuming an existing recurring charge has stopped.
