Skysay sends a small number of emails about your workspace. They come from
**Skysay &lt;hello@skysay.ai&gt;**, and a reply reaches our support team. Each
email is written in your sign-in language (English, German, Spanish,
Estonian, French or Russian; other languages receive English).

Emails go only to a member's **verified sign-in address**, never to an
address typed into a form or call. A member who leaves the workspace, or
whose address changes, stops receiving them.

## Account emails

| Email | Sent to | When |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome | The person who signed up | Once, after the workspace is created and the terms are accepted |
| Funds added | Workspace owners | After each paid wallet top-up, including auto-recharge. Promotional credit does not send one. Stripe still sends the payment receipt. |

## Reminders

Reminders point at something that still needs your attention. Each one is
checked again immediately before it is sent, so you are never reminded of
something you have already done.

| Reminder | Sent to | When |
|---|---|---|
| Create your first agent | The person who signed up | 24 hours after signup, if the workspace still has no agent |
| Number waiting for payment | Workspace owners | When a phone number request has waited 24 hours for its first payment |
| Number needs verification | Workspace owners | When a phone number request has waited 24 hours for identity or company verification that you still need to complete. Not sent while a check is in progress or Skysay is reviewing it. |
| We miss you | Workspace owners | When a workspace with an agent has had no dashboard visit and no real call for 14 days; once per quiet period |

A workspace receives **at most one reminder every 48 hours**, whatever the
reason. A number request is reminded about payment or verification at most
once. Reminders cover only activity after reminders were switched on for
Skysay; nothing earlier is ever reminded.

These emails are about your own workspace and are not marketing. Email open
and link clicks are measured to improve them.
