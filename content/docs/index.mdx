Skysay is **agent-native telephony**: real phone numbers, calls, and SMS
that an AI agent can run on its own. Connect a supported client through **MCP**, or use the **API** from your own
application. Public docs explain setup, permissions and the tools your
credential can use.

<Callout title="Start with a read-only connection">
  Use `https://api.skysay.ai/mcp/read-only` with `account:read` and the recipe
  for your actual client. Verify an authenticated `account_overview` result
  before enabling phone actions.
</Callout>

## What is Skysay?

Skysay gives your agents the phone side of the world: search number
coverage, wire an agent to a number, place an authorized outbound call, read
recordings and transcripts, and send SMS — all behind one scoped API key that is
also your permission boundary. Numbers, compliance, billing, and provider
operations stay on the Skysay side; your agent gets the capability tools and
nothing else.

Looking for a number first? [Phone numbers for AI agents](/phone-numbers-for-ai-agents)
introduces numbers in 100+ countries for personal calls, businesses, and apps,
with MCP, API, and workspace setup options. Availability, supported features,
and registration requirements depend on the number you choose.

## Connect your client

Use hosted HTTP MCP directly. Start with a narrow credential and a read-only
check; you do not need a private source checkout or local bridge. Choose the
recipe matching your client:

Read-only access does not provision numbers or contact people. Enable a billable
workflow only after reviewing its permissions, funding, policy and exact human
approval. Browser audio tests and real phone calls have different funding and
activation requirements.

### Do I need a Twilio account?

No. For Skysay-managed numbers, Skysay handles the carrier side and onboarding.
You still provide any required identity, business information and documents;
number activation is not instant in every market.
[Number requirements](/docs/regulated-numbers) explain that process.

### Does the framework's model speak on the phone?

Not automatically. The SDK or workflow operates Skysay's tools. A published
Skysay voice-agent flow or your supported audio integration handles the
conversation. [Wire an agent](/docs/wire-an-agent) explains the options.
