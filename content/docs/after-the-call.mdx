After a call with a conversation, Skysay writes a short AI summary of it. You
read it in **Calls**: in the list's Goal and Summary columns, and at the top of
the call's details. What it is judged against and which facts are pulled out of
each call are set on the AI agent, in its **After the call** section.

Every field in the summary is an AI estimate. Skysay never uses it to retry a
call, block a number, bill or change do-not-call. The call's status, who
answered (a person, voicemail or a phone menu) and its duration are platform
facts and are not part of the summary.

## What the summary says

| Field | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Summary | One to three sentences. |
| Follow-up needed | Yes or no, with one line saying what still has to happen. |
| Goal met | Yes, partly, no or unclear, with one line saying why. |
| Sentiment | How the other person seemed: positive, neutral, negative or unclear. |

The summary is free and on for every AI agent, existing ones included. It is
made by Google Gemini from the call transcript after the call ends, and it is
written in English, whatever language the call was in. A call
that reached a voicemail greeting or a phone menu is summarised from what was
transcribed, and the summary says that no person took part.

## Set the call goal

Open **Agents**, choose the AI agent and select **After the call**.

**Call goal** is one optional line, for example "Find out if the store has the
item, its price and today's closing time." Goal met is judged against it. When
it is empty, Goal met is judged against the AI agent's own instructions, as
published, never against the values a single call filled in. The call details
say which one was used: "Judged against your call goal" or "Judged against the
AI agent's instructions".

A goal line is at most 4,000 characters. A change applies to calls whose
transcript arrives after you save; calls already summarised keep their summary.

## Turn the summary off for one AI agent

In **After the call**, switch **AI call summary** off and save. Calls this AI
agent makes or takes from then on get no summary. Summaries already written
stay until their transcript is deleted.

## Read summaries on the Calls page

The list has two columns for it:

- **Goal**: Met, Partly, Not met or Unclear. A dash means the call has no
  summary.
- **Summary**: the summary itself, with "Follow-up needed." added when one is.

Open a call and the **Details** tab starts with the **Call summary** card: the
summary, chips for goal met, follow-up and sentiment, the follow-up line, why
the goal was or was not met, and what it was judged against. On a phone, each
row of the list repeats its goal and the start of its summary under the row. The AI agent's
result fields follow directly under it. The call facts, recording and
transcript come after them.

A call that has no summary says why:

| Shown | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Summarising… | The transcript has arrived and the summary is being written. The page updates on its own. |
| Not answered | The call never connected (no answer, busy, or it failed). No summary is made. |
| No conversation | The call connected but the other side did not speak. No summary is made. |
| Not enough transcript | The other party spoke, but too little of it was transcribed to summarise. |
| Summary failed | The summary could not be written. |
| No summary | The AI agent has summaries off, or the call is older than call summaries. |

Summaries are deleted with the call's transcript, when the recording is
deleted or its retention ends.

The same summary is available to code with `GET /v1/calls/{call_id}/summary`
and the MCP tool `get_call_summary`; see the [generated API
reference](/docs/api-reference).

## Result fields

Result fields are facts Skysay pulls out of every completed call for this AI
agent, such as whether the store had the item or the price it quoted. They are
shown in the call's details and sent in the `call.extraction.ready` webhook
(see [Post-call results](/docs/post-call-results)). They are an AI estimate
too, kept apart from the summary, and free.

They used to be set as raw JSON on the Integrations page. They are now edited
in the AI agent's **After the call** section; existing fields were kept as
they are, and the Integrations page links to each AI agent's section.

For each field you set:

- **Name**: letters, digits and underscores, at most 80 characters. Your
  systems receive the value under this name.
- **Type**: text, number, whole number, or yes / no.
- **What to look for**: a description the AI reads, at most 600 characters.
- **Choices** (optional, for text): the only values the field may take,
  separated by commas. At most 50, each at most 120 characters.
- **Required**: whether every result must include it.

An AI agent has up to 24 fields. **Use a template** adds a starting set (store
errand, appointment, lead qualification, payment reminder, support triage);
it adds only fields you do not have yet. **Edit as JSON** shows the same fields
as a JSON schema, for pasting or bulk edits.

Each save that changes the fields creates a new version. Calls already
analysed keep the version they used; a save that changes nothing creates no
version. Removing every field and saving turns result fields off for new calls.
